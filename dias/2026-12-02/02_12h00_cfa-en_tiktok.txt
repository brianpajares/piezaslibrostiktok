🎯 Nominal vs. real return

3 lessons from the book:
1⃣ Nominal return: What you earn before inflation.
2⃣ Real return: What you earn after inflation: your real purchasing power.
3⃣ Quick approximation: Real ≈ nominal − inflation. Exact: (1+nominal)/(1+inflation) − 1.
